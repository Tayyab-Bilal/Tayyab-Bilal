# 💫 About Me:
👋 About Me<br><br>I’m a **Senior Machine Learning Engineer** with 4+ years of experience building **production-grade AI systems** across **LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), agentic workflows, and unsupervised learning**.<br><br>My work sits at the intersection of **ML research and scalable systems**—from designing **agent orchestrators** that automate complex data ingestion and reporting, to developing **high-accuracy clustering pipelines** that turn unstructured user data into actionable insights with minimal manual effort.<br><br>I’ve built and deployed systems involving **LLMs, vector databases, cloud-native architectures, and real-time pipelines**, with hands-on experience in **NLP, computer vision, multimodal RAG, and audio-based AI workflows**. I care deeply about **embedding quality, evaluation metrics, system reliability, and cost-performance tradeoffs** when taking ML from experimentation to production.<br><br>Currently, I’m focused on **agentic AI systems**, **scalable RAG architectures**, and **unsupervised methods for structure discovery in messy real-world data**, while continuously exploring better ways to design reliable, interpretable, and efficient AI systems.<br>
